# CDCP Widget — LinkedIn Lead-Magnet Campaign

*9 posts. One every 5–7 days (~7 weeks). Psychologically sequenced — do not compress or reorder.*
*Voice: Pillar 2. Compressed. Declarative. Operator vocabulary. The math is the pitch.*
*Every post: demo link goes in the FIRST COMMENT, never the caption → `https://sss.marketing/embed/demo.html`*
*DM responses: run the playbook (`CDCP-WIDGET-ONBOARDING-PLAYBOOK`). Reply within the hour while the post is warm.*

**Reader journey:** name the problem (1) → prove it with one number (2) → make the offer (3) → repeat it in French for Quebec (4) → then one segment per post feels personally seen: coordinators (5), front desk (6), patients/mission (7), DSO ops (8) → close the arc with receipts (9).

## Post 2 — The Number Nobody Explains

**Target:** owners + treatment coordinators. **Trigger:** curiosity → authority. The save-this-post post.
**Strategic purpose:** demonstrate command of the math before the tool exists publicly. This is the receipt for everything after.

> "100% covered" still costs money. Here's the math no one shows patients.
>
> Quebec. New-patient exam and cleaning. ACDQ 2026 fee guide: 344 $.
>
> CDCP doesn't pay the fee guide. It pays its own federal grid: 268 $.
>
> Patient at the 100% tier:
> CDCP pays 268 $. Patient owes 76 $.
> Fully covered. Still pays 76 $.
>
> Same visit in Ontario: $423 fee, $342 grid.
> The 60% tier patient pays $217.80 — co-pay plus the gap.
>
> That gap has a name: balance billing. Legal. Standard. And almost never explained before the patient is in the chair.
>
> Surprise is what kills trust at the front desk. The clinics that explain the gap before the visit keep the patient. The ones that don't, explain it during checkout.

**CTA:** *"Want the math for your province? Ask below."* Answer every comment with real bundle figures — each reply is a public demo.
**Graphic headline:** `« Couvert à 100 % ». Still owes 76 $.`
**Visual:** the widget's ledger table, cropped — fee, CDCP pays, gap, patient share. Real numbers, tabular, no decoration.

**Strategic purpose:** owns the CDCP's *second-year* problem: patients who were covered last year and lapse because they didn't renew. Positions the calculator as a tool that keeps patients covered, not just one that converts them. Sits outside the 1–9 sequence; it runs on the calendar.

> Nobody told them. That's the whole problem.
>
> The free CDCP calculator we install on clinic websites now tells every eligible patient this before it costs them anything: three steps, the June 1 deadline, the June 30 cliff.

## Operating notes

- **First comment, every post:** `Try it as a patient would → https://sss.marketing/embed/demo.html` (post 4: add `?lang=fr`).
- **Founder comment strategy:** one substantive self-comment within 10 minutes of posting (extra math, the verbatim front-desk script, a province someone asked about). Replies > likes; answer every province question with real figures from the engine.
- **Visual system:** all assets from the live widget via `kit.html` recolors — screenshots, not illustrations. Ink/cream brand frame around every graphic.
- **Numbers discipline:** every dollar figure in these posts is from the shipped engine (QC 344/268 → 76 $; ON 423/342 → $81 gap, $217.80 at 60%). If a bundle rate changes, update post copy before publishing.
- **DM → call:** qualifier reply templates and the 15-minute call script live in the playbook. Log every onboarded clinic — post 9 depends on it.
