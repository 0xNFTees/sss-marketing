01Free eligibility check

Do you qualify, and how much will you pay?

An estimate, not a decision. Only Service Canada confirms eligibility, and your dentist confirms the fee.

02Who qualifies

Four requirements, all of them.

These are the federal requirements on canada.ca. Your spouse or common-law partner has to meet the tax ones too.

No dental insurance
No access to private coverage

Not through a job, a pension, a family member's plan or a health spending account. Having access counts, even if you don't use it.

Income
Under $90,000

Adjusted family net income: line 23600 for you and your spouse or partner, adjusted for UCCB and RDSP amounts.

Taxes
Last year's return filed

You, and your spouse or common-law partner if you have one.

Residency
Canadian resident for tax purposes

Also applies to your spouse or common-law partner.

A provincial or territorial dental program does not disqualify you. If you have both, the plans coordinate so nothing is paid twice.

03What you pay

Your share depends on family income.

CDCP pays a percentage of its own established fee for each covered service. The rest is your co-payment.

Your dentist's fee can be higher than the CDCP fee. When it is, you pay the difference on top of any co-payment. Ask the clinic for an estimate before treatment.

04What's covered

Most everyday care is covered, and some of it needs approval first.

The covered services, as canada.ca lists them. Items marked * require preauthorization: your dentist asks CDCP before treatment, and not every request is approved.

Preventive

  • Exams, including emergency exams
  • X-rays
  • Cleaning (scaling)
  • Fluoride
  • Sealants

Basic

  • Fillings
  • Root canals (re-treatment*)
  • Cleaning under the gumline
  • Treating abscesses
  • Gum disease management

Major

  • Crowns*
  • Complete dentures
  • Partial dentures*
  • Denture repairs and relines
  • Extractions and oral surgery

Sedation

  • Nitrous oxide and oral sedation
  • Conscious sedation*
  • General anesthesia*

Orthodontic services, such as braces, are currently not available under CDCP. Implants, whitening and veneers are not on the covered list.

05Apply and renew

Coverage runs July 1 to June 30 and does not renew on its own.

Apply

Applications for the 2026–27 benefit period are open. Apply online on canada.ca or by phone at 1‑833‑537‑4342.

Get your coverage date

Once approved, you get a coverage start date. Book after that date: care received before it is not covered. Sun Life processes CDCP claims for the government.

Renew each spring

In 2026, renewal ran April 15 to June 1. Members who did not renew lost coverage on June 30 and can reapply, with a gap in coverage.

Care received while you are not covered is not reimbursed later.

06In Quebec

RAMQ pays first for what it covers.

For children under 10 and people on financial assistance with a claim booklet, the dentist bills RAMQ for RAMQ-covered services. CDCP can cover the services RAMQ does not, such as cleanings, if the patient is eligible.

08Questions

What people ask about CDCP.

Is the Canadian Dental Care Plan free?
It is free to apply and renew. Care is fully covered at CDCP fees only if your adjusted family net income is under $70,000. Between $70,000 and $89,999 you pay a 40% or 60% co-payment, and any clinic fee above the CDCP fee is also yours.
Who qualifies for CDCP?
Canadian residents for tax purposes with no access to private dental insurance, an adjusted family net income under $90,000, and last year's tax return filed, for themselves and their spouse or common-law partner.
How much will I pay at the dentist with CDCP?
Your co-payment is 0%, 40% or 60% of the CDCP fee depending on family income, plus any difference between your dentist's fee and the CDCP fee. The calculator on this page estimates it for a typical visit.
Does CDCP cover braces, Invisalign or implants?
No. Orthodontic services are currently not available under CDCP, and implants are not on the list of covered services.
Does every dentist accept CDCP?
No, but most do: 29,762 oral health providers participated as of October 31, 2026. Ask the clinic before booking, or use the provider search on Sun Life's CDCP site.
Can my dentist charge more than CDCP pays?
Yes. CDCP fees are often below provincial fee guides. If the clinic's fee is higher, you pay the difference, in addition to any co-payment.
When do I need to renew CDCP?
Every year, in the spring. In 2026 the renewal window ran April 15 to June 1, and coverage for those who did not renew ended June 30.
I missed the renewal deadline. What now?
You can apply again for the 2026–27 benefit period, but you will have a gap in coverage, and care received during the gap is not reimbursed.
