The Through-Line

You hired us to help generate new patients. Now we help clinics retain, reactivate, and manage them more efficiently. Every dollar of capital extends the same relationship deeper into the clinic's operations — acquisition first, then lifecycle, then the systems that run both. Infrastructure that compounds, not expenses that evaporate.

05Physical MoatThe NFC Pad

Software doesn't have this.

A 60mm disc on the front desk. The patient taps; the phone opens a clinic-branded portal; one tap later a Google review is filed. The pad is the moat — physical placement that compounds Maps rank, lowers the cost of the next patient, and survives ad-platform churn.

01

Physical placement.

On the front desk, in the patient's hand at checkout. No app to install. No friction the patient has to invent a reason to overcome.

02

Review velocity.

Each tap routes through a clinic-branded portal that filters for satisfaction, then files the review on Google before the patient leaves the chair.

03

Compounding cost-per-patient.

Reviews lift Maps rank. Maps rank lifts organic share of search. Organic share cuts the spend required to win the next patient. The loop closes monthly.

04

Zero clinic workload.

No staff training. No content production. No on-camera dentist. The clinic delivers dentistry; the pad delivers the marketing flywheel.
